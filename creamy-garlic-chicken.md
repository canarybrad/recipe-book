# Creamy Garlic Chicken

## Ingredients
- 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 1.3 lbs total)
- 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp freshly cracked black pepper
- 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 2 Tbsp butter, divided
- 1 whole garlic bulb (about 8-9 garlic cloves)
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 3/4 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions
1. Fillet each chicken breast into two thinner cutlets (or use thin-cut breasts). Season with Italian seasoning, salt, and black pepper. Coat both sides evenly with flour.
2. Heat a large skillet over medium heat with olive oil and 1 Tbsp butter. Cook chicken on each side until golden brown and cooked through (about 4 minutes per side). Remove to a clean plate and cover.
3. Peel garlic cloves and smash them with the side of a chef's knife by pressing with the heel of your hand.
4. Add remaining 1 Tbsp butter to the same skillet. Add garlic cloves, reduce heat to medium-low, and sauté for 3 minutes until lightly browned and fragrant. Stir frequently to prevent burning.
5. Pour in chicken broth and heavy cream. Add garlic powder. Stir and scrape up brown bits from the pan bottom. Simmer 8-10 minutes until sauce reduces by about 50%.
6. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper as needed (approximately 1/8 tsp each).
7. Return chicken to the skillet and spoon creamy sauce over top. Heat through and serve with fresh chopped parsley (optional).

## Notes
- Option to use boneless, skinless chicken thighs instead.
- For minced garlic alternative, use 2.5 Tbsp pre-minced garlic.
- For thicker sauce: add roux of 2 Tbsp flour and 1 Tbsp melted butter, or a cornstarch slurry.
- Suggested additions: caramelized onions, bacon, spinach, mushrooms, lemon juice, or Parmesan cheese.
- Serve with mashed potatoes, pasta, rice, or salad.
- Prep 10 min, cook 30 min, serves 4.
