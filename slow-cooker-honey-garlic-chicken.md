# Slow Cooker Honey Garlic Chicken

## Ingredients
- 1½ pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs or chicken breasts
- ⅓ cup low-sodium soy sauce
- ⅓ cup honey
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 2 teaspoons chili paste (sambal oelek, sriracha, or hot sauce)
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
- 2 tablespoons cornstarch
- Toasted sesame seeds (for serving)
- Chopped green onion (for serving)

## Instructions
1. Place chicken in slow cooker. Whisk together soy sauce, honey, tomato paste, chili paste, garlic, and rice vinegar. Pour over chicken.
2. Cover and cook on LOW for 4-5 hours or HIGH for 2-3 hours, until internal temperature reaches 165°F. Flip chicken halfway through if possible.
3. Remove chicken to a plate to cool slightly. Whisk cornstarch into the liquid in the slow cooker. Cover and cook on HIGH for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until sauce thickens.
4. Shred chicken and return to slow cooker. Stir to coat with sauce. Serve over rice topped with green onions and sesame seeds.

## Notes
- To thicken sauce on the stovetop instead, transfer sauce to a saucepan and cook over medium heat for 5-10 minutes.
- Refrigerate up to 4 days; freeze up to 3 months.
