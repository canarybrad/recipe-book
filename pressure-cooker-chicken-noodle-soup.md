# Pressure Cooker Chicken Noodle Soup

## Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon butter
- 1 cup diced onion
- 4 carrots, peeled and cut into 1/4-inch rounds
- 1 celery rib, diced
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 6 cups chicken broth or stock
- 1 sprig fresh thyme
- 1 tablespoon dried parsley
- 1 teaspoon salt
- Freshly ground pepper to taste
- 2 cups diced chicken (cooked or uncooked)
- Egg noodles, cooked separately according to package directions

## Instructions
1. Select Sauté mode on the pressure cooker and melt butter. Add onion, carrots, celery, and garlic. Cook about 3 minutes, stirring occasionally.
2. Pour in chicken broth and scrape up any browned bits from the bottom of the pot. Add chicken, salt, pepper, parsley, and thyme.
3. Lock the lid and cook on High Pressure for 4 minutes.
4. When the cook time ends, let the pressure release naturally for 5 minutes, then quick-release any remaining pressure.
5. Remove and discard the thyme sprig. Taste and adjust seasoning.
6. Ladle the soup over cooked egg noodles in individual bowls and serve.

## Notes
Cook the egg noodles separately so they don't get mushy or soak up too much broth — especially helpful for leftovers.
